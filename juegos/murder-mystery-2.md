# Murder Mystery 2

**Genre:** social deduction · **Style family:** classic dark grid ·
**Why:** #1 by concurrent players in August 2026 (538.7K on Aug 20; all-time peak 1.35 M per StudioKrew's tracker).

The lesson: **the rarity name bar** — every item tile ends in a colored strip with its name, so a grid of 50 knives is readable at a glance.

## Visual language

| | Observed |
|---|---|
| Tiles | Dark gray squares (`#54574D`), item render centered, count `x62` top-right. |
| Name bar | Full-width strip at the bottom of each tile, colored by rarity: Godly **magenta** (`#F507B1`), Ancient red, Legendary green, etc. |
| Tabs | Dark tabs (`#2C2B27`) with white text; active is lighter. |
| Font | Bold sans (Source Sans look). |

## Community redesigns

Modern dark-purple redesigns of the MM2 inventory (sidebar categories, rarity under the name, gradient `Deposit` button)
circulate as portfolio pieces. They show where the genre is going but are **not** the shipped UI.

## Steal this

- Rarity strip under every item name — works for any trading game.
